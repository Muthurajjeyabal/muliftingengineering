About Us

Trusted Lifting Engineering Consultancy

Delivering safe, compliant and efficient lifting solutions for complex industrial projects worldwide.

MU Lifting Engineering is a specialized international consultancy providing professional Crane Lift Planning, Critical Lift Planning, Rigging Engineering and Offshore Lifting support.

With over two decades of hands-on experience as an LEEA Appointed Person and Offshore Crane Operator, we deliver methodical, standards-compliant lift plans and engineering studies that protect people, assets and schedules.

We serve clients across Oil & Gas, Offshore, Construction, Petrochemical, Renewable Energy, Marine and Industrial sectors.
