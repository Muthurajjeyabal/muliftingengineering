About Us

Trusted Lifting Engineering Consultancy

Delivering safe, compliant and efficient lifting solutions for complex industrial projects worldwide.

MU Lifting Engineering is a specialized international consultancy providing professional Heavy Lift Planning, Rigging Engineering, and Offshore Lifting support. We deliver methodical, standards-compliant lift plans and engineering studies that protect people, assets, and project schedules.

Led by an experienced Heavy Lift Engineer / Offshore Lifting Specialist and LEEA Appointed Person, we integrate deep field experience with advanced digital calculation tools to solve complex lifting challenges across the Middle East, Europe (North Sea), and Africa.

Our core expertise covers Oil & Gas, Wind Energy, Petrochemical, and Marine sectors — delivering Critical Lift Plans, Rigging Studies, and Method Statements aligned with LEEA, BS, API, and DNV standards. Independent technical reviews for complex offshore and onshore lifting operations.
